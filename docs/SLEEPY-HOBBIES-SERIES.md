# OddHobb Sleep — Series Specifications

> Niche sleepy documentaries. One channel. "[NICHE] to Fall Asleep To."
> Content builds audience → audience buys products → products advertise channel.

---

## The Channel

**Name:** OddHobb Sleep (or "Sleepy Hobbies")
**Tagline:** "[NICHE] to Fall Asleep To"
**Format:** 1-3 hour sleepy documentaries
**Voice:** Calm, warm, 0.7x pace, no urgency
**Frequency:** 3-5 episodes per week

## The Flywheel

```
Niche discovered → Sleepy long-form video → Enthusiast/search audience
→ Comments reveal more product ideas → Etsy products
→ Products advertise channel → Channel advertises products
```

## Three Content Formats

### 1. Sleepy History
"The Quiet History of [X]"
- History of Mahjong
- History of Fountain Pens
- History of Model Railways
- Forgotten Chess Clubs of Victorian Britain

### 2. Sleepy Workshop
"[Activity], Slowly"
- Building a Mechanical Watch Slowly
- Binding a Book from Start to Finish
- Wiring a Bonsai Tree
- Tying 30 Trout Flies

### 3. Sleepy Catalogue
"[Number] [Adjective] [Niche] [Objects]"
- 100 Beautiful Fountain Pen Inks
- 50 Types of Bonsai
- 100 Vintage Playing Card Decks
- 50 Classic Dice Games

**Catalogue videos are the killer format.** Low narrative pressure, endless objects, extremely sleep-friendly, and they map directly onto products.

## Production Pipeline

### Per Episode (3-5 hours)

1. **Research** (30 min) — Reddit, Etsy, hobby forums
2. **Write script** (1-2 hours) — calm narration, no urgency
3. **Record voiceover** (1-2 hours) — 0.7x pace, warm tone
4. **Add visuals** (30 min) — Wikimedia images, product photos
5. **Edit** (30 min) — OddHobb logo intro + product outro
6. **Upload** (15 min) — YouTube + Spotify

### Per Episode Output

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| **Length** | 1-3 hours |
| **Voice** | Calm, warm, 0.7x pace |
| **Background** | None or very low ambient |
| **Visuals** | Slideshow, one image per topic |
| **Intro** | "OddHobb Sleep — [NICHE] to Fall Asleep To" |
| **Outro** | "We make strange little things for people who love [NICHE] — OddHobb." |
| **Description** | Product links + SEO tags |

## The Selection Loop

YouTube becomes a demand probe:

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Video gets 200k views | Produce more products in this niche |
| Products sell well but video doesn't | Keep commerce, don't scale media |
| Both blow up | Discover a whole OddHobb vertical |
| Neither works | Kill the niche, move on |

**Same research cost produces content + product research + SEO intelligence.**
