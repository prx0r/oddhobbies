# OddHobbies Brand Guide

## Voice & Tone

### We Sound Like
- A hobbyist who found a better way
- Practical, not precious
- "This fits X" not "revolutionary"
- "Designed to solve Y" not "game-changing"

### We Never Sound Like
- Corporate
- Medical/therapeutic
- Condescending ("even beginners can...")
- Generic ("high quality, great value")

## Naming

### Format
`[Product] — [What It Fixes]`

### Examples
- "Adjustable Angle Jig — Holds 2mm-8mm Glass"
- "Wire Spool Tower — Collapses Flat for Storage"
- "Color Library 24 — Vertical Strip Organizer"
- "Powder Sweeper — Cleans Board Gutters"

### Avoid in Titles
- Brand names (Scrabble, Rummikub, DMC, Diamond Art Club, Breyer)
- Medical language
- Superlatives ("best", "ultimate", "perfect")

### Use in Titles
- Dimensions ("fits tiles up to 15mm", "holds 2mm-8mm glass")
- Compatibility ("fits standard 6-strand floss", "1:9 scale")
- Quantity ("set of 10", "24-strip organizer")
- Hobby-specific terms (cross stitch, bonsai, quilling, etc.)

## Visual Identity

### Colors
| Color | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Warm White | #FAF8F5 | Backgrounds |
| Charcoal | #2C2C2C | Text |
| Terracotta | #C4654A | Accents |
| Sage | #7A9E7E | Secondary accent |
| Kraft | #C9B99A | Containers, tags |

### Typography
- **Headings:** DM Sans (clean, modern)
- **Body:** Inter (readable)
- **Accents:** Caveat (hand-drawn feel)

### Photography Style
- Product on clean surface (wood, linen, kraft paper)
- Hands holding/using the product (scale reference)
- No busy backgrounds
- Natural light preferred
- Show the hobby context (e.g., glass pieces next to the jig)
