## The Pattern That Wins

> **"Mass-market accessory exists, but enthusiasts have weird geometry."**

Bonsai pot dimensions, telescope rails, glass angles, airbrush valve shapes, leather patterns, lathe shaft diameters, rock specimen shapes — all cases where conventional manufacturing dislikes SKU proliferation and **3D printing loves it**.

**Hunting heuristic:** Find the hobby where practitioners have a table covered in things that don't quite fit, and the "proper" version is either too expensive or doesn't exist.
