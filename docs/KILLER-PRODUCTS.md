## Personalization Strategy

**Every product should offer at least one of:**
1. **Name engraving** — Player name, family name, pet name
2. **Color choice** — Match their game/team/aesthetic
3. **Theme selection** — Fandom, zodiac, element, guild
4. **Custom text** — Short phrase, inside joke, date

**Personalization adds $5-15 to price with ~$0.50-1.00 extra cost.**
