## Key Insights from Today

1. **Google AI search is the new SEO.** The 8 product feed attributes are real and most merchants are missing them.

2. **Pinterest rewards consistency.** Pin daily, 5 designs per product, keyword-rich titles.

3. **AI agents are the new customers.** Build structured data they can read. Q&A pairs + Document Links + knowledge graphs.

4. **Transparency is the marketing.** Show all suppliers, link to them. People trust honest shops.

5. **One quality > multiple tiers.** Simpler, more honest, better margins.

6. **Dropship from multiple suppliers.** No inventory risk. JSON defines what's in each pack.

7. **The content flywheel works.** YouTube → audience → Etsy sales → more content → more audience.
