## What OddHobb Is

**OddHobb = a studio for people who are really into things.**

Not an Etsy shop. Not a YouTube channel. Not a product catalog. A studio that:
1. Discovers niches with intense, tactile, collecting, identity-driven communities
2. Makes sleepy content about those niches
3. Sells products for those niches
4. Uses content to discover the next niche
5. Builds an asset vault that retains customers forever

**The tagline:** *Stuff for people who are really into stuff.*

**The promise:** Every custom OddHobb creation is yours to keep — physical and digital.

## The Vault

Every purchase includes:
- Physical product
- Downloadable digital asset (STL/GLB/SVG/PDF)
- Perpetual personal-use licence
- Saved in OddHobb Vault
- Reusable on future compatible products

**The moat:** OddHobb remembers the weird hobby-specific thing we already made for you.

**The compounding effect:**
```
Christmas 2026: Dad's Fisherman cribbage pegs
  → avatar created once

Birthday 2027: Golfer ball-marker (same avatar)
Christmas 2027: Champion trophy (same avatar)
Later: Desk shrine (same avatar)

Creative cost: ONCE.
```

## The Neurodivergent Gift Pack Angle

**Projects as gift packs = perfect for neurodivergent people.**

Why:
1. **Structure** — Clear steps, clear outcome, no ambiguity
2. **Tactile** — Physical making engages the senses
3. **Collectible** — "I made this" feels better than "I bought this"
4. **Identity** — "I'm the person who builds cyberdecks"
5. **Completion** — Finished project = dopamine hit
6. **Reusability** — Vault assets can be used again
7. **Giftability** — "Build your own X" is a better gift than "X"

**The gift pack concept:**
```
ODDHOBB PROJECT 004
Bookbinder's Pocket Book Kit
Version 1.2

Includes:
• Physical jig set
• SVG cutting templates
• PDF instructions
• STL files
• Tutorial video access
• Saved to your OddHobb Vault
• Perpetual personal-use licence

Perfect for: the person who always wanted to try bookbinding
```

**This is not "a product." This is a PROJECT you can give someone.**

## The Complete Flywheel

```
1. DISCOVER niche (probes, Pinterest, Reddit, Etsy)
2. RESEARCH demand (what do they search for, what do they buy)
3. DESIGN products (physical + digital)
4. SOURCE suppliers (Makr3D, Prodigi, Kunaki)
5. CREATE content (tutorials, sleepy videos)
6. PUBLISH content (YouTube, Spotify)
7. LINK content to products (descriptions, end screens)
8. SELL products (Etsy, Gumroad, Amazon)
9. INCLUDE digital assets (Vault)
10. RETAIN customers (Vault = reason to come back)
11. ANALYZE data (what sold, what content performed)
12. DISCOVER next niche → repeat

Same research cost produces:
• Content (YouTube, Spotify)
• Product research (what to build next)
• SEO intelligence (what people search for)
• Customer insight (what they actually buy)
```
