### How It Works
1. Upload STL or 3MF file
2. Get instant quote (real slice, not estimate)
3. Create product in Makr3D
4. List on Etsy at your price
5. Paid orders sync automatically
6. Makr3D prints, checks, packs, ships
7. Tracking writes back to Etsy

### Key Features
- **Faithful 3MF mode** — prints your exact slice settings, not their defaults
- **AI Print Intelligence** — checks geometry/overhangs before production
- **Human QA** — every order inspected before shipping
- **Protective design licensing** — other sellers can list your design with your permission, you earn royalty
