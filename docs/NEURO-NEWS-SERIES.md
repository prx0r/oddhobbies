# Neurodivergent News — Series Concept

> ADHD, autism, AuDHD, PDA research and stories. Gateway to DivergentJoy + OddHobb products.

---

## The Concept

A series about neurodivergent lives — not clinical, not inspirational. Just real stories, real research, real experiences. The audience is neurodivergent people and the people who love them.

**Format:** 8-15 minute episodes, themed around specific topics
**Voice:** Warm, personal, slightly wry
**Frequency:** 2-3 episodes per week

---

## Why It Works

| Factor | Why |
|--------|-----|
| **Audience size** | 15-20% of people are neurodivergent — massive market |
| **Content gap** | Most content is clinical or "inspirational" — nobody does warm + real |
| **Community** | Neurodivergent people share content aggressively when it resonates |
| **Product fit** | Natural tie-in to DivergentJoy products (planners, cards, tools) |
| **Spotify** | Perfect format — listen while doing other things |

## Episode Format (10-15 minutes)

```
[LOGO INTRO — 2 seconds]

[HOOK — 0:30]
"One thing that surprised me about [topic] is..."

[STORY — 2-3 minutes]
Real example. Not clinical. Personal or from research.

[EXPLANATION — 3-5 minutes]
What's actually happening in the brain. Plain language.

[WHAT HELPS — 2-3 minutes]
Practical strategies. Not "just try harder."

[PRODUCT MENTION — 0:30]
"I made a [product] for exactly this."

[OUTRO — 0:30]
"Your brain isn't broken. It's different. And different can be a superpower."
```
