## The Problem to Solve

Needlepoint has exploded but the **finishing bottleneck** is real:
- New stitchers can't find supplies
- Experienced stitchers can't finish fast enough
- Project management is chaos (threads, scissors, needles, WIPs)
- Display of finished pieces is an afterthought

**Don't sell canvases. Sell the tools around the hobby.**

## Why This Works

1. **Proven demand** — Pinterest +1,351%, Etsy +175%, Reddit community exploding
2. **Underserved** — No one is making tools for the new stitcher bottleneck
3. **Gen-Z audience** — Young people rediscovering old hobbies
4. **Cheap to make** — Makr3D prints cost $1-4
5. **Content pipeline** — 500+ years of needlework history to narrate
6. **Gift market** — Perfect for stitchers who "have everything"
