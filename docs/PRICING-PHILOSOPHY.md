# OddHobb — Pricing Philosophy

> Super cheap. Fairly priced. Premium feel.
> The digital layer costs us nothing to include. Give it away.

---

## The Rules

1. **Never overcharge.** If it costs £2 to make, sell it for £8-12, not £30.
2. **Always include the digital asset.** It's free for us to provide.
3. **Make it feel premium** through packaging, presentation, and the Vault — not through inflated prices.
4. **Underpromise, overdeliver.** "You get the physical thing AND the 3D file AND the licence AND it's saved to your account."
5. **The reaction should be:** "Why is this so cheap for what I'm getting?"

## The "Why So Cheap?" Effect

When someone buys a £7.99 line reader and also receives:
- The STL file
- The GLB preview
- A perpetual licence
- It's saved to their OddHobb Vault
- They can reuse it on future products

They think: **"This is insane value."**

And it costs us almost nothing extra, because the digital asset was generated once and can be included infinitely.

**The physical object is the hook. The digital layer is the magic. The Vault is the reason they come back.**
