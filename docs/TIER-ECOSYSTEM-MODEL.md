# OddHobb — The 3-Tier Hobby Ecosystem Model

> Tutorial → Digital Assets → Physical Product → Repeat.
> Saved verbatim from research.

---

Here's where this gets dangerous. The model is:
Tutorial → Digital Assets → Physical Product → Repeat
One pipeline, three revenue streams, applied to any hobby where people make things.

## Tier 1 — Massive Demand, Existing Digital Markets

| Niche | Digital Assets | Physical Product | Why It Works |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|--------------|
| **D&D/TTRPG** | STL minis, terrain, props | Printed minis, DM screens, dice towers | Biggest tabletop hobby on earth. People pay for both "make it" and "buy it." |
| **Warhammer** | STL proxies, terrain, bits | Printed proxies, paint racks, hobby tools | Insanely loyal spending. $500+ per army. |
| **Chess** | Piece STLs, board templates | Custom pieces, pedestals, trays | Universal game. Gift market huge. |
| **3D Printing itself** | STL files, mods, upgrades | Print accessories, enclosures, upgrades | Meta-hobby. People buy tools to make things. |
| **LEGO custom** | Custom minifig STLs, MOC instructions | Printed custom parts, display cases | LEGO community is massive and pays premium. |
| **CNC/Woodworking** | SVG files, DXF cuts, CNC paths | Physical signs, boxes, furniture parts | Established digital marketplace (Etsy SVG sellers do 6-figures). |
| **Leathercraft** | Pattern PDFs, stamps, templates | Custom goods, tool rolls, wallets | Patterns are the #1 digital product in leathercraft. |
| **Sewing/Quilting** | Pattern PDFs, fabric prints | Custom garments, quilt kits | Pattern market is enormous. Indie pattern designers thrive. |

## The Meta-Play: Hobby Ecosystems

Don't just do one product. Build the entire digital + physical ecosystem for each hobby:

**DUNGEON MASTER ECOSYSTEM:**
├── Tutorials (YouTube, free)
│   ├── "How to build a dungeon tile"
│   ├── "Painting minis for beginners"
│   └── "Creating terrain on a budget"
├── Digital Assets (Etsy, $5-20 each)
│   ├── STL pack: dungeon tiles ($12)
│   ├── STL pack: treasure chests ($8)
│   ├── SVG: monster tokens ($10)
│   └── PDF: encounter planner ($6)
├── Physical Products (OddHobbStudio, £15-50)
│   ├── Printed terrain pieces
│   ├── Custom DM screen
│   ├── Initiative tracker
│   └── Dice tower
└── Bundles (premium, £40-80)
    ├── "DM Starter Pack" (digital + physical)
    └── "Campaign Kit" (everything)

## The Revenue Math

One hobby ecosystem (e.g., D&D):
- 20 tutorials on YouTube (free)
  → 50K views/month × $10 RPM = $500/month AdSense
- 30 digital assets on Etsy
  → 100 sales/month × $10 avg = $1,000/month
- 10 physical products on OddHobbStudio
  → 50 sales/month × £25 avg = £1,250/month
- Patreon for early access
  → 100 members × $5 = $500/month

**Total: ~$3,250/month from ONE hobby ecosystem**

Scale to 5 ecosystems = $16,000/month

## My Recommendation: Start With D&D

Why:
1. Biggest tabletop hobby on earth — millions of players
2. Existing digital market — STL files sell like crazy
3. Gift market — "gift for D&D player" is a massive search
4. Your products already fit — dice towers, mini plinths, DM screens
5. Content demand — people search "D&D terrain tutorial" constantly
6. Community — Reddit, Discord, TikTok are all huge
7. AI tools ready — Meshy generates minis, Claude writes tutorials
