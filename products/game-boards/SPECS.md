## Production Notes

### 3D Printed Boards
- PLA+ for structure
- Laser engraving for personalization
- Felt bottom for grip
- Magnetic closure for folding boards

### Wood Boards
- Source blank boards from Alibaba/AliExpress
- Laser engrave in-house
- Hand-finish with oil/stain
- Add felt bottom and hinges

### Neoprene Mats
- Print via print-on-demand (Prodigi or similar)
- Custom artwork uploaded to printer
- Roll and ship in tube
