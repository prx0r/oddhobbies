## Photo Strategy

### Per Listing
1. Hero shot on kraft/linen background
2. In-use shot (hand holding product near fabric)
3. Scale reference (product next to coin/ruler)
4. All variants/colors shown
5. Packaging/what's included
6. Detail close-up (texture, magnet strength)
7. Lifestyle shot (on crafting desk)
8. Size diagram with measurements

### Mockup Tools
- Canva (free, good for flat lays)
- Real photos (best for trust)
- 3D renders (for unstitched products)
