## Why not 3D Vikings as “the US supplier”
Marketing sounds US-friendly, but **print farm is in Riga, Latvia** (SIA 3D Factory).  
US orders = international shipping + possible customs for buyer. Fine for EU; not the US answer.

## Why Printie for US
- **US facility** — domestic transit
- Etsy + Shopify sync
- **No monthly fee**, pay per order
- **One flat fee** covers print + finishing + shipping
- White-label packaging / branded unboxing
- Qty 1 — no MOQ
- Tracking writes back to store

## Why Makr3D still matters
- Best-documented in our stack (costs £1.29 / £2.50 / £7.60)
- Native Etsy order sync
- Human QA, shipper of record
- Worldwide from day one
- White-label packing slip with our brand
- Instant quote from real slicer

| Mid | **£2.50 (~$3.37)** | tile rack, winds |
| Large | **£7.60 (~$10.30)** | cribbage board, big trays |

Printie: **flat fee per print includes shipping** — quote each SKU after upload; expect competitive on small desk toys once shipping is bundled.
