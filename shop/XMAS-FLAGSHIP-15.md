## Album product (the banger)

**Personalised Memory Album** — customer sends fun facts, stories, style notes per track → we produce a **4–10 song EP/album** + **cover art** + **CD** + **digital download**.

**Wedding Video Album** — customer sends long wedding videos → we compile a **keepsake CD** + optional **layflat photo book**.

**Why weddings / anniversaries / Xmas:**
- High emotional spend, low price sensitivity
- Etsy wedding templates already prove demand (11k+ pattern in our notes)
- Photo books / calendars / mugs are proven POD gift formats
- Our edge: **custom audio production**, not just photo layout
- US + UK labs (Printful US, Prodigi/Kunaki/DiskCrafters)
